ICS-FOD Foreign Object Detection System

What it does

An AI engine, camera, and alarm system in one platform

The ICS-FOD is an AI system that provides continuous, real-time detection of foreign objects on conveyor belts in mining and mineral processing operations. By combining an AI engine, embedded computing device, industrial-grade camera, and a configurable alarm and integration platform, ICS-FOD identifies hazardous objects before they reach critical downstream equipment — preventing costly belt damage, equipment failures, and unplanned production downtime.

Deep Learning Vision

Detects ferrous & non-ferrous tramp metal and oversized objects — classes missed by conventional metal detectors.

< 1s Latency

From object entering camera FOV to alarm output — instant belt stop on confirmed detection.

24 / 7 Monitoring

No blind spots between inspections. Operates overnight and during shift changes with no operator input.

How it works

From detection to belt stop

1

Camera watches the belt

The industrial-grade camera continuously monitors the conveyor line, feeding video to the embedded AI engine.

2

AI classifies objects in real time

The deep-learning model, trained on mining-specific data, identifies tramp metal and oversized objects as they enter the frame.

3

Alarm fires in under a second

On confirmed detection, the configurable alarm and integration platform triggers — including an instant belt stop.

4

Downstream equipment is protected

Hazardous objects are caught before reaching critical downstream equipment, preventing belt damage and unplanned downtime.
