HOW TO CONNECT icsmongolia.com TO CLOUDFLARE
=============================================

These steps tell Squarespace to hand over control of your domain's internet
traffic to Cloudflare. It should take about 10 minutes to do, then up to
2 hours for the change to take effect worldwide.

You will not lose your domain — it stays registered in Squarespace. You are
only changing where the traffic gets directed.

---

STEP 1 — LOG INTO SQUARESPACE
------------------------------
1. Go to squarespace.com and sign in to your account.
2. In the left menu, click "Settings".
3. Click "Domains".
4. Click on "icsmongolia.com" to open its settings.


STEP 2 — FIND THE NAMESERVERS SECTION
--------------------------------------
1. Scroll down until you see a section called "Nameservers" or a button
   that says "Use Custom Nameservers". Click it.
2. You will see a list of nameservers that look like addresses ending in
   ".googledomains.com". These are the old ones you need to remove.


STEP 3 — REMOVE THE OLD NAMESERVERS
-------------------------------------
Delete all four of these (they may not all be shown, just remove what is there):

   ns-cloud-e1.googledomains.com
   ns-cloud-e2.googledomains.com
   ns-cloud-e3.googledomains.com
   ns-cloud-e4.googledomains.com


STEP 4 — ADD THE NEW CLOUDFLARE NAMESERVERS
--------------------------------------------
Type in these two new addresses exactly as shown below, one per field:

   randall.ns.cloudflare.com
   zara.ns.cloudflare.com


STEP 5 — SAVE
--------------
Click Save (or Confirm, or Update — the button label may vary).
Squarespace may show a warning that your site could be affected. That is
normal. Click through to confirm.


STEP 6 — TURN OFF DNSSEC (if you see it)
------------------------------------------
While you are in the domain settings, look for a setting called "DNSSEC".
If it is turned ON, please turn it OFF and save. This prevents a conflict
with Cloudflare. (If you do not see this setting, you can skip this step.)


STEP 7 — WAIT
--------------
The change can take anywhere from a few minutes to 2 hours to take effect.
You do not need to do anything else during this time.

Once Cloudflare confirms the domain is active, the website will be live at
https://icsmongolia.com


---
If you have any trouble or are unsure about any step, please reach out before
making changes. It is always safe to stop and ask.
